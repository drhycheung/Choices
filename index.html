    EASTER EGG 彩蛋 · 一念之差

    You found this. Three things worth remembering. One: there is no randomness inside a run — your ending is not luck, it is the fifteen choices you made, one after another. Two: the story is not a straight ladder. Two of your decisions send you into a consequence scene that other players never see, so two people can start the same venture and be asked different questions. Three: each of the six ventures hides exactly one path that finishes with all three risk lines at zero and all three venture boxes at 7 or above. Find it and a gold badge appears on the ending screen. 你找到了这里。记住三件事。一、一局之内没有任何随机数——你走到这个结局不是运气，是那十五个选择一步步推出来的。二、故事不是一条直线：其中两个决定会把你带进别人看不到的后果场景，两个人做同一门生意，会被问到不同的问题。三、六个题材里，各藏着唯一一条「三条风险线全为零、创业三格都在 7 分以上」的路。走到的人，结局页会出现一枚金色徽章。
